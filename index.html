Our Courses

Complete Quran Reading, Tajweed & Noorani Qaida

Step-by-step mastery of the Arabic alphabet, articulation points (Makharij), vowel markings (Harakat), essential Tajweed rules, and fluent Mushaf reading (Nazira) from basics to fluency.

Noorani Qaida: Arabic alphabet, articulation points (Makharij) & Harakat
Tajweed Rules: Essential pronunciation rules with clear examples
Quran Reading (Nazira): Fluent, mistake-free Mushaf recitation

Why Choose Us

Dedicated personal mentorship, clear age eligibility, and transparent monthly fee policies.

Interactive Live Online Classes

Classes will be held 3 days a week for steady, structured progress.

Separate Batches & Eligibility

Separate batches for kids, adults, and women. Ages 6+ for Quran, Tajweed & Noorani Qaida; Essential Fiqh & Duas is open to everyone (under-15 kids & adults).

Monthly Fee Policy & Progress Tests

Regular monthly evaluations. Under the monthly plan, ₹400 must be paid at the beginning of each month to attend that month's classes; if unpaid, classes for the next month will not be available until paid.
